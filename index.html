Our story

A community school for Kotturu

Wisdom Wings English Medium School is run by Taata Education Trust (R), Bannigola, a registered trust first registered in July 2019 (Document No. HBH-2019-20) and rectified in December 2025. The school is located at Siddaganga Nagara, 19th Ward, Itagi Road, Kotturu, in Vijayanagara District.

The school is affiliated to the Central Board of Secondary Education, New Delhi (Affiliation No. 831768, School Code 47452) and follows the CBSE curriculum with English as the medium of instruction.

The trust runs the school as a community service and not as a business. No part of the school's income is diverted to any individual; savings are used only to develop the school.

Our Vision

To nurture confident, curious and responsible young people who carry strong values and a love of learning into everything they do.

Our Mission

To provide quality CBSE education in English to children of Kotturu and nearby villages, in a safe and caring campus, with qualified teachers who know every child.

How we assess

Assessment Framework

We follow CBSE guidelines and the National Education Policy 2020. Assessment is continuous and looks at the whole child: understanding, skills, effort and attitude, not just marks in an exam.

Pre-primary

Play-based learning under our nursery teachers. Progress is recorded through observation of language, numbers, motor skills and social behaviour. No formal examinations.

Classes I – V

Regular class work, oral and written tests, projects and activities. Two terms each year, with a progress report at the end of each term.

Classes VI – VIII

Periodic tests, notebook submission, subject enrichment activities and portfolios, along with half-yearly and annual examinations as per CBSE.

Co-curricular

School Houses

Every student belongs to one of four houses. Houses compete in sports, quizzes, debates, cultural programmes and cleanliness drives through the year, building teamwork, leadership and school spirit.

Co-curricular

Saturday Activities

Saturdays are for learning by doing. Students take part in activities that build creativity, fitness, values and life skills, alongside a shorter academic schedule.

Yoga & Sports

Yoga, drills and outdoor games led by our physical education teachers.

Art & Craft

Drawing, painting, clay work and craft projects.

Music & Dance

Group songs, patriotic songs and folk dance practice.

Clubs

Reading, science, eco and quiz clubs that run house-wise.

Values & Life Skills

Moral stories, health and hygiene talks, and road safety.

Public Speaking

Assembly presentations, elocution and debates in English.

A safe, well-equipped campus

Our campus covers 4,653.885 sq. m on Itagi Road, Kotturu. The school building has a ground floor and three upper floors, and is certified safe by the Public Works Department and the Karnataka State Fire and Emergency Services.
